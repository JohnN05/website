## Overview

Every registration season, UMD students cross-reference two sites: Testudo's
Schedule of Classes for seats and sections, and PlanetTerp for professor
ratings and GPA history. Terp Rater merges them — it injects ratings, GPA
data, and live seat/waitlist counts directly into Testudo's own course
listings, so the second tab is unnecessary.

Live on the Chrome Web Store: 51 users, rated 5.0★ from 6 ratings.

## How it works

A Manifest V3 Chrome extension, ~935 lines across 8 JavaScript modules,
integrating two REST APIs — PlanetTerp (`/api/v1/course`,
`/api/v1/professor?reviews=true`) and Testudo's SOC (`/soc/{semester}/sections`).

- **Living inside someone else's page.** Testudo re-renders its listings
  asynchronously, so a `MutationObserver` in the content script re-applies
  the injected data as the DOM changes.
- **Request coalescing.** One professor teaches many sections on a page,
  and sections process concurrently via `Promise.all`. A result cache plus
  an in-flight request map means a miss *awaits the existing promise*
  instead of firing a duplicate call — one professor, one request, no
  matter how many sections they teach. PlanetTerp is a free community API;
  being polite to it is a feature.
- **Graceful degradation.** A 5xx or network failure from PlanetTerp shows
  a banner instead of failing silently, and a per-course try/catch keeps
  one bad course from breaking the page.
- **Minimal permissions.** Scoped `host_permissions` and a custom content
  security policy — the extension asks for exactly what it needs.
- **The features themselves:** average GPA and rating badges with a review
  modal, seat/waitlist highlighting, sort by rating or open seats, filter
  out full sections, and a bookmark manager persisted in
  `chrome.storage.local`.

## Result

Shipped in August 2024 and still maintained — version 0.0.2, last updated
July 2026, nearly two years later. 51 students use it during registration,
and the listing collects no data and shares nothing with third parties.
Apache-2.0, known issues documented in the README.
