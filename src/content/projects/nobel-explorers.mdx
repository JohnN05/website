## Overview

Nobel Explorers is a global technology-education initiative. I joined as
a facilitator while still in high school. Over eight months I taught 60+
international students across 3 cohorts, in Ukraine, Taiwan, and Brazil
among others, teaching a curriculum covering machine learning, computer
vision, and internet fundamentals.

## What I did

- **Instruction**: I led cohorts through the ML/CV curriculum remotely,
  across time zones and language barriers, for students seeing this
  material for the first time.
- **Program expansion**: I helped launch new educational programs
  internationally, growing the initiative beyond its existing footprint.

## Why it stuck

This is the experience my education minor points back to. Teaching 60+
people how computers learn, before I'd taken a single college CS course,
is what made Secondary Education feel less like an elective and more like
continuing something I'd already started. It's also why "I build software
people actually use, then teach the next person how to build it too" is
on the front page of this site.
