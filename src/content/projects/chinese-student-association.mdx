## Overview

As CSA's Auxiliary Officer Representative, I manage a cohort of 20–25
Auxiliary Officers (the people who staff and run the organization's
events) and own the pipeline that develops them.

## What I do

- **Recruitment**: I lead the annual AO recruitment cycle, from
  application review through selection.
- **Event ownership**: I coach AO teams to plan and run three AO-led
  events on their own, building the experience that prepares them for
  future Executive Board roles.
- **Operations**: I run weekly meetings that coordinate people and
  resources for CSA's flagship events, the Lunar Banquet and Chinatown
  Cafe, which draw up to 400 attendees.

## What it's taught me

The job is leadership without being the headline. My events succeed when
twenty-five other people are effective, not when I am. Delegating real
ownership (a whole event, not just a task list) turns out to be the
same skill as running a project board. It just comes with higher stakes
and worse version control.
