## Overview

As CSA's Auxiliary Officer Representative, I manage and oversee a cohort
of 20–25 Auxiliary Officers — the people who staff and run the
organization's events — and own the pipeline that develops them.

## What I do

- **Recruitment** — lead the annual AO recruitment cycle, from application
  review through selection.
- **Event ownership** — coach AO teams to independently plan and execute
  three AO-led events, building the experience that prepares them for
  future Executive Board roles.
- **Operations** — facilitate weekly meetings coordinating people and
  resources for CSA's flagship events — the Lunar Banquet and Chinatown
  Cafe — which draw up to 400 attendees.

## What it's taught me

The job is leadership without being the headline: my events succeed when
twenty-five other people are effective, not when I am. Delegating real
ownership — an entire event, not a task list — turns out to be the same
skill as running a project board, just with higher stakes and worse
version control.
