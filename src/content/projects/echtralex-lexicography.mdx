## Overview

Full write-up in progress. Built with a team of 5 for CMSC435, in
collaboration with Echtralex founder Michael Horlick — automating a fully
manual bilingual (Chinese/English) transcript-alignment workflow that
previously took 8–20 minutes per document by hand.

The client measured roughly a 100% speedup against the old workflow, and
the system held ~98% alignment accuracy. Full story soon: the greedy
matcher we threw out, the LaBSE + custom-scoring redesign that replaced it,
and the HTML extraction work I owned directly.
