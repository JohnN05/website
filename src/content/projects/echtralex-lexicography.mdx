## Overview

Full write-up in progress. Built with a team of 5 for CMSC435, in
collaboration with Echtralex founder Michael Horlick — automating a fully
manual bilingual (Chinese/English) transcript-alignment workflow that
previously took an average of 12 minutes per transcript by hand.

The client measured a ~30% efficiency gain — 12 minutes down to 8.5 per
transcript — across 10 real ministry transcripts, faster on 9 of the 10,
with typically just 1–2 manual corrections needed per ~40-sentence
transcript. Full story soon: the greedy matcher we threw out, the LaBSE
model I researched, selected, and built the accuracy test harness for (a
teammate implemented the alignment algorithm itself), and the HTML
extraction work I owned directly.
